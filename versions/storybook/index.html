CaseHuddle

PII scrubber.

2025–Present

Co-founder & Lead Full Stack Engineer

Detection, review, and redaction built into clinical case writing.

  1. 01

    The problem

    Case drafts can contain identifying patient details. The review flow needs to flag them without stripping useful clinical context.

  2. 02

    My role

    I integrated a self-hosted GLiNER model and built the application logic for filtering detections, reviewing matches, and redacting selected text.

  3. 03

    The decisions

    • Mapped names, organizations, addresses, emails, phone numbers, dates of birth, and Social Security numbers into seven typed review categories.
    • Added TypeScript heuristics, per-category thresholds, and configurable filters to tune predictions and reduce false positives.
    • Resolved overlapping spans and merged adjacent matches. Replacements run backward through the text to preserve character offsets.
    • Let authors review title and body matches, replace them with placeholders such as [NAME], or keep false positives. Repeated mentions share a decision.
    • Made edits invalidate an earlier review, and recorded overrides and failed checks for moderator follow-up.
  4. 04

    The result

    Authors can review and redact flagged details before publishing. Fixture-based verification reports missed and unexpected detections; a comparison tool evaluates different label sets.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

GLiNER · TypeScript · Entity spans · Confidence thresholds · Human review · Fixture-based evaluation
